A functional guide for Handshake users

An HNS faucet gives away small amounts of Handshake coins, so you can get started with the network before buying any.

Request your first HNS coins

Note: This faucet gives you 20 HNS, which is enough to get you started: bid on a name, win it, and then set up a site following any of the guides. If after testing you want to buy more coins, you can do so on Neoxa, a non-KYC exchange.

How it works

  • You must have joined the Handshake DNS community, otherwise the transaction won't go through.
  • Once you've joined, fill in the form below with your HNS address and your Discord username.
  • If your Discord username is in the community's member list, your address will receive 20 HNS.

Some considerations

You must join the Handshake DNS Discord, otherwise the coins will never be sent. Coins are sent in batches every 24 hours, after a human check. To prevent abuse of the faucet, each username and address can only be used once. Obviously, someone could use another person's username, but if I notice suspicious behaviour, or if the limit of 2,000 HNS per month is reached, I might change the process and ask users to send a DM to prove they control their Discord account. For now I don't think that's necessary, so it's better to keep things simple.
